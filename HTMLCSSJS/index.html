Endemic Food Security

Ensete ventricosum (False Banana)

Known as the "Tree Against Hunger," Enset is a vital staple for over 20 million people. Its majestic stature and resilient nature make it a symbol of agricultural heritage.

Indigenous

Coffea arabica

The birthplace of coffee. Wild arabica plants still thrive in the shade of Ethiopia's montane forests, representing a genetic goldmine for the world's most popular beverage.
